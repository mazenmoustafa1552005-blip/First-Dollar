# Organic distribution

## Accounts (from research/organic-acquisition.md, revisit after 90 days)
- Instagram: one crochet account, one miniatures account. Mystery games: Pinterest + occasion-timed search content first; add a third IG account at about 5 games.
- One Pinterest account with boards per line is fine (search engine, not a feed).

## Each post
Hook, storyboard reference, caption, hashtags (5-8 relevant), cover, destination link with UTM: `?utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=<SKU>`.

## Calendar
`analytics/content-calendar.csv` (date, line, SKU, platform, format, asset path, link, status, post URL). Status values: idea, ready, scheduled (only with scheduler evidence), posted (only with post URL).

## Communities
Follow each subreddit/group's self-promotion rules (log them in organic-acquisition.md). r/crochet allows linking your own pattern to a shop since Feb 2026, not to social media.

## Scheduler
Metricool MCP only after the owner connects it and authorises posting. Before that, export ready-to-post packages. Never claim a post is scheduled without the scheduler's returned ID.

## Measure
Weekly: product visits, outbound clicks, orders per channel into `analytics/weekly.csv`. Expand formats and SKUs that show clicks/orders, not only views.
