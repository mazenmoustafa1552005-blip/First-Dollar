# Bubbles the No-Sew Whale (CRO-SEA-01)

> DRAFT: not yet crocheted or tester-checked. Size and yardage are estimates.

Terminology: US crochet terms

## Materials
- Worsted weight (US 4 / aran-ish) cotton or acrylic yarn: main color about 30 to 40 m (estimate, confirm on sample)
- Small amount of contrast yarn for the fins (optional, can be main color)
- 3.5 mm (E-4) crochet hook
- Pair of 8 mm safety eyes (for children under 3, embroider eyes instead)
- Polyester fiberfill stuffing, stitch marker, yarn needle for weaving in ends only

## Gauge and size
Gauge is not critical. Work tightly so stuffing does not show. Expected finished size about 11 to 13 cm long (estimate, to be measured on the sample).

## Abbreviations
- **MR**: magic ring
- **sc**: single crochet
- **inc**: 2 sc in the same stitch
- **dec**: single crochet decrease (invisible decrease recommended)
- **hdc**: half double crochet
- **dc**: double crochet
- **sl st**: slip stitch
- **ch**: chain
- **st(s)**: stitch(es)

## Body (worked in continuous rounds from the nose)
- **R1:** 6 sc in magic ring (6)
- **R2:** inc x6 (12)
- **R3:** (sc, inc) x6 (18)
- **R4:** (sc 2, inc) x6 (24)
- **R5:** (sc 3, inc) x6 (30)
- **R6:** (sc 4, inc) x6 (36)
- **R7-R12:** sc 36 (36). 6 rounds. After R9, insert safety eyes between R9 and R10, about 8 sts apart.
- **R13:** (sc 4, dec) x6 (30)
- **R14-R15:** sc 30 (30). 2 rounds.
- **R16:** (sc 3, dec) x6 (24)
- **R17:** sc 24 (24). Fasten the eye backs now. Begin stuffing firmly.
- **R18:** (sc 2, dec) x6 (18)
- **R19:** (sc, dec) x6 (12). Finish stuffing the body. Leave the last part lightly stuffed.
- **R20:** sc 12 (12)
- **R21:** (sc, dec) x4 (8)
- **R22:** sc 8 (8). Do not stuff the tail.

## Tail fluke (worked in rows straight off the body, no sewing)
- **Row 1:** sc 4 through both layers (4). Flatten the opening so the stitches line up in 4 pairs. Work each sc through one stitch of the front and one of the back. Ch 1, turn.
- **Row 2:** inc x4 (8). Ch 1, turn.
- **Row 3:** ((hdc, dc) in next st, dc in next st, (dc, hdc) in next st, sl st in next st) x2 (12). Fasten off and weave in the end.

## Fins (make 2, crocheted directly onto the body)
- **Fin:** ch 4 (4). Hold the whale nose up. Join yarn with a sl st around a stitch of R12 on one side, level with the eye and 3 rounds below it.
- **Fin, continued:** sc in 2nd ch from hook, hdc in next ch, dc in last ch (3). Sl st around the next stitch of R12 to anchor. Fasten off, pull the tail inside the body. Repeat on the other side.

## Assembly
- There is no sewing of parts: the tail is worked straight off the body and the fins are crocheted onto it.
- A yarn needle is used only to weave in ends.
