# Start here: The Corner Grocer Pantry Set

1. Pick the file for your scale: 1-6 (fashion dolls), 1-12 (classic dollhouse), 1-16, or 1-24 (half scale), and your paper: A4 or Letter.
2. Print at 100% / Actual size. Turn OFF "Fit to page", "Shrink oversized pages" and "Scale to fit".
3. Measure the ruler at the bottom of the page: the bars must be exactly 50 mm and 2 inches. If not, check your printer dialog before printing more.
4. Page 1 of each file lists every item and its finished size, the line key (cut, fold, glue) and assembly steps.
5. Use 160 to 200 gsm (60 to 75 lb) matte card for boxes and regular paper for can labels.
