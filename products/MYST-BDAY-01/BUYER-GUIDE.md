# Start here: The Case of the Vanishing Birthday Cake

Thank you for your purchase! (This text is the buyer guide; it is also used as the download note on each platform.)

1. Choose your version: Color or InkSaverBW (black and white), and A4 or Letter (US). The clues are identical in every version.
2. Print the HostGuide for yourself. Do not show it to the players: it contains the solution.
3. Print the PrintPack: the 2-page case file once per detective or team, everything else once.
4. Follow "Before the party" in the host guide (about 15 minutes: cut cards, fill 3 envelopes).
5. On the day, follow the run sheet: opening script, 3 evidence rounds, timed hints at minutes 15, 30 and 40, accusations, solution.

You need: printer, scissors, 3 envelopes, pencils, a timer. "Fit to page" printing is fine for this game.
