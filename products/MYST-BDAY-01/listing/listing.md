## Description
Grandma Rosa's lemon birthday cake was on the kitchen counter at 3:00 pm. At 3:20 pm it was gone. Six suspects. Twelve clues. One sticky solution.

The Case of the Vanishing Birthday Cake is a printable detective game for kids aged 8 to 12. Your guests become detectives: they study suspect cards, open three envelopes of evidence, fill in their case file and make an accusation. The host reads a short script and hands out the clues. No acting, no costumes, no reading lines.
