# Start here: The Midnight Snack Mystery

1. Choose Color or InkSaverBW, and A4 or Letter. The clues are the same in every version.
2. Print the HostGuide for yourself only: it contains the solution.
3. Print the PrintPack: the 2-page case file once per detective or team, everything else once.
4. Follow "Before the party" in the host guide: cut the cards and fill 3 envelopes (about 15 minutes).
5. Read the opening script, open one envelope per round, and offer hint cards at minutes 15, 30 and 40 if needed.

"Fit to page" printing is fine. License: personal and family use; please do not share or resell the files.
