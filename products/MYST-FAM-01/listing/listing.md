## Description
Mom baked 12 cookies for the school bake sale. At 7:00 am only 6 were left. Everyone says they were fast asleep, but somebody in this house had a midnight snack and is not telling the truth.

The Midnight Snack Mystery is a printable detective game for families with kids aged 8 to 12. Players study six suspect cards, open three envelopes of evidence, check the house map and tick suspects off their case file until only one is left. The host reads a short script and hands out clues. The answer key and hints are all in the host guide.
