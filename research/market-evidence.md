Studio: individual seller, international English market, organic marketing only.
Research date: 2026-11-14. All observations below were made on this date.

## How to read this file (method and limits)

- **Page fetching was blocked.** Direct page fetches failed for www.etsy.com, www.ravelry.com and www.nightofmystery.com (host not allowed in this session's network). After three hosts failed the same way, no more direct fetches were attempted. Every figure below comes from **search-engine result snippets and summaries** (WebSearch), not from a page I opened. Treat each figure as "seen in search results on 2026-11-14, not verified on the live page".
- **Signals are kept separate.** Column meanings:
  - *Listing reviews* = review count shown next to a listing in search results (for Etsy this is often the shop-wide count, which Etsy shows on listing cards; this is flagged where ambiguous).
  - *Shop sales* = "Sales N" on an Etsy shop page.
  - *Shop reviews* = shop-level average and count.
  - *Views/carts* = "X views in the last 24 hours" or "in N carts" text that appeared as a search result title.
  - *Ravelry* = projects, queues, rating votes.
  - "n/c" = not captured in the snippet. It does not mean zero.
- **Seller names** are given only where the snippet named them. Where a snippet gave a title and price but not a URL, the source search page is noted and the URL is marked "not captured".
- Prices are in USD unless stated. Etsy snippets mixed currencies (USD, CAD, EUR, MXN); a "sale" price is a temporary discount, not the list price.

### Line 1 observations

- **Price bands (seen):**
  - Etsy single games, indie sellers: mostly **$6-16** (cluster at $7.99, $9.74-12.99, $15.44). Floor sale prices $2-5.
  - Etsy bundles: 3-game kids bundle $24.51 (list $32.68), so roughly $8 per game.
  - Personalized/custom: $12.99 (personalized case file) to $109 (custom, up to 20 kids).
  - Publisher sites (My Mystery Party, Red Herring): **$29-49** for a full-cast download.
  - Free lead magnets exist (Midnight Manor, Instant Detective Game, Rainy Day Detective Kit on Payhip, Moms and Munchkins "Mixed Messages").
- **Buyer occasions seen in listings:** birthday party (most common: "missing cake", "missing donuts"), classroom activity, sleepover/tween party, family game night, Christmas, Halloween, Easter, after-school/camps/libraries (Night of Mystery B2B angle). **Summer** as an explicit theme was not seen in any listing found.
- **Complaints / friction (from review snippets and reviews of comparable kits):**
  - Prep and printing load: "you will spend time printing and preparing" (Room Escape Artist, home kit); "printing ~20 pages of materials, cutting out puzzle pieces" (kids kit review). Sources: https://roomescapeartist.com/2020/08/10/escape-kit-mysterious-murder-hivemind-review , https://roomescapeartist.com/2022/04/06/paper-adventures-case-aunties-manor-kids-product-review/
  - Duration longer than stated (Etsy reviewer, item 2).
  - Difficulty mismatch: clues "too easy (solved in 10 seconds) or too hard" (escape-kit.com guide, https://escape-kit.com/en/diy/murder-mystery-for-kids/); pieces too small for kids.
  - Age-range confusion: listings say 7-12, 8-13, 8-12, 8-14 for similar games; My Mystery Party ages its kid games at 7-9 or 10+, rarely 8-12.
  - License: publishers require a commercial license for paid/public events.
- **Differentiation gaps:**
  1. **Explicit 8-12 band with two difficulty tiers** (only seen on 6-10 products, items 10-11).
  2. **Low-print / black-and-white mode**: no listing found advertising an ink-saving B&W version or a page count. Prep burden is the main complaint.
  3. **Short setup**: "low prep" is claimed, but no listing states setup time in minutes (for example "15-minute setup").
  4. **Summer and family-night specific titles**: not found.
  5. **Flexible player counts** (5-10 vs 6-14 vs 8-32): small-family versions (3-5 players) are rare; item 7 covers it in its host guide.
  6. **Mid-price gap** between Etsy ($8-15) and publishers ($29-49): a "complete party" tier at $15-20 (invitations, name tags, certificates, host script) has few examples.

### Line 2 observations

- **Price bands (seen):**
  - Small single sets (one product type, labels, bags): **$1-5**.
  - Mid packs (45-65 pieces, themed box sets): **$5-10**.
  - Room boxes / shop kits: **$10-15** (top seen $14.71).
  - A community comment notes "some charge up to $8 for a one-page printable" (r/Dollhouses thread, see Part B), and there was criticism of printable prices in that subreddit.
- **Bundle pricing:** large bundles are heavily discounted (65+ designs at $2.21; 40-50% off snack "MEGA bundle" on 1:6). Discounting is the norm on Etsy, so list prices are inflated before sales.
- **Buyer occasions / uses:** dollhouse kitchens and shops, Barbie play (1:6, often parents and kids), Blythe and other play-scale dolls, dioramas, holiday-themed room boxes.
- **Complaints / friction:**
  - Scale: "The designs are big for 1:12 scale" (Etsy review on a printable shop); seller note: "Some prints can turn out a bit bigger than expected". One shop's layout is 8.5 x 11 portrait only.
  - Materials: glossy photo paper makes ink tear at folds; watery glue blurs inkjet ink and buckles thin paper.
  - Files too large for home printers at 1:12 (CGTrader buyer).
- **Scales seen vs gaps:**
  - 1:12: abundant.
  - 1:6 (Barbie): common for food packaging and fast food, but **no Barbie-scale printable shop/shelf kit found**.
  - **1:24, 1:48: no printable grocery/bakery/cafe kit found.** Only advice to "set your printer to 50% to achieve 1:24".
  - **1:16 (Sylvanian/Calico Critters/Lundby-adjacent): no printable shop found.** Calico Critters are often said to fit 1:12; Lundby is 1:18.
  - One generic page claims a seller ("Print mini") offers all of 1:6, 1:12, 1:24 and 1:48, but I could not confirm it (the source page looked like scraped content).
- **Differentiation gaps:**
  1. **Multi-scale files**: each kit with pre-sized pages for 1:6, 1:12, 1:16, 1:24 and 1:48 instead of "scale it yourself".
  2. **Complete shop sets** (shelves + signage + stock + bags + price tags) rather than packaging only. Shelving printables were not found.
  3. **US Letter and A4** versions, plus a printed scale ruler on every page to check the print.
  4. **Paper and glue guidance** (matte paper weight, glue type) to head off the main complaints.
  5. **Printer-friendly option**: a low-ink or light-background version (no listing advertises one).

### Line 3 observations

- **Price bands (seen):**
  - Single pattern: **$2.50-5.15** typical (Ribblr, Amigurumi.com, LoveCrafts, Payhip). Outliers: $1.00 beginner and $6.99 two-version turtle.
  - Small bundles (3-7 patterns): price mostly not captured.
  - Large bundles: $7.84 (11-in-1 no-sew, 280 reviews) to $29.99 (10-in-1, not no-sew). Deep-discount "40 patterns" at $2.40.
  - **Free competition is heavy**: Sweet Softies, Briana K, Vivcrochets, Repeat Crafter Me and many Ribblr and Ravelry patterns are free no-sew sea animals. Paid value has to come from ad-free PDF, tested quality, sizes, video and bundling.
- **Buyer occasions:** quick gifts, keychains, baby/premature-baby toys (Crazy Patterns listing for preemies), market-stall makers selling finished items (license terms matter: most allow selling finished items with credit).
- **Complaints / friction (amigurumi generally; no no-sew-specific complaints were found):**
  - "There are mistakes in the pattern and it's not very clear to follow" (2-star, Etsy SoftLoop, Jan 26, 2026), from https://www.etsy.com/at/shop/softLoopofficial
  - Finished piece "didn't match the photo".
  - Language-only patterns (Italian only) leading to 1-star reviews.
  - Advanced patterns with no-refund policy; unclear chart rounds (Crazy Patterns forum).
- **Differentiation gaps:**
  1. **A visually unified no-sew sea collection** where all pieces share one style and scale (most bundles mix styles; several are "low-sew" rather than no-sew).
  2. **Beginner-tested, tech-edited** patterns with a published error-free and tester note (errors are the top complaint).
  3. **Video per pattern** (free competitors use video as their hook; paid PDFs rarely bundle it).
  4. **Size options** (keychain / plush), as Octobuddy does with 3 sizes.
  5. **US and UK terms** in one file, and a clear "sell your finished items" license.
- **Search-interest signals (Trends MCP; Google Search and Pinterest, values 0-100):**
  - "crochet octopus": Google -22% (12M, 36 to 28); Pinterest -27% (12M, 70 to 51).
  - "no sew crochet": Google -33% (12M, 48 to 32); -59% over 3M (78 to 32), so interest peaked around August 2026. Pinterest data unavailable.

### Top differentiation gaps per line

- **Mystery games:** an explicit ages 8-12 band with two difficulty tiers; a stated low page count with a B&W print option; a stated setup time (for example "15-minute setup"); summer and family-night titles (none found); a small-family mode (3-5 players).
- **Dollhouse:** **multi-scale in one purchase** (1:24, 1:48 and 1:16 shop kits were not found at all; 1:6 shop/shelf kits were not found); full shop sets including shelving and signage; a print-check ruler, plus paper and glue guidance.
- **Crochet:** a stylistically unified, truly no-sew sea collection; tested and tech-edited (answers the top complaint); a video for each pattern; sizes from keychain to plush.
